# Research

## What is it?
Investigate ChatGPT behavior, product capabilities, limitations, sources, and test results.

## Core idea
Research starts with a question, records its sources and dates, and distinguishes observations from interpretations.

## How to use this section
Document the question, method, evidence, limitations, and next steps. Do not label an untested proposal as a verified capability.

## Example question
What does current official documentation confirm about a ChatGPT feature?
